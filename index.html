What it does

Press Play. Embercube handles the rest.

Every loader

Vanilla, Fabric, Quilt, Forge and NeoForge, installed for you. Forge support starts at 1.13.

Java, handled

Each version gets exactly the Java runtime it needs, downloaded on first launch. No installers, no PATH fiddling.

All your accounts

Sign in with Microsoft once, switch in a click, and pick which account each instance plays as. Run two games side by side on different accounts. Tokens are encrypted with a key kept in your OS keychain.

Modrinth built in

Browse mods, modpacks, resource packs and shaders filtered to your instance. Required dependencies come along automatically.

Instances that share

Every instance is its own folder, but libraries, assets, Java and identical mod files are stored once and shared.

Updates you can undo

See which mods, resource packs and shaders have updates, read the changelogs, update everything in one click, and roll back if something breaks.

Checked before Play

Missing dependencies, wrong versions, mods that clash and duplicates are spotted before the game starts, each with a one-click fix.

A crash doctor

If the game crashes, Embercube reads the logs for you, names the mod responsible and offers the fix. When the log can't tell, a mod search finds the culprit in a few runs.

Worlds and servers

Back up and restore worlds, with optional automatic backups after each session. See your servers live with player counts, and jump straight into any world or server.

Skins and capes

Keep a library of skins with a 3D preview, wear one in a click and switch capes, without opening a browser.

Big Picture mode

A full-screen library you drive with a controller, for the couch or a Steam Deck. Add any instance to Steam or to your desktop as a shortcut.

Bring your instances

Import from Prism Launcher, MultiMC, the Modrinth App and CurseForge without touching the originals. Export any instance as a Modrinth modpack.

Authentication, security & game ownership

Your account stays yours. Your game stays legit.

Signed in by Microsoft, not by us

You sign in on microsoft.com with a short code. Embercube never sees your password. It follows the same Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft services chain as the official launcher.

Tokens never leave your computer

Sign-in tokens are encrypted on disk with a key that lives in your system's keychain: macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager or the Linux Secret Service. There are no Embercube servers and no analytics. Removing an account deletes its tokens.

You need to own Minecraft

Signing in checks that your Microsoft account has a Minecraft: Java Edition profile, and online play needs it. Embercube doesn't sell, unlock or bypass the game. Offline profiles are for singleplayer and LAN only, and online-mode servers reject them.

Official files, checked

The game and Java come straight from Mojang (or Adoptium), and mods come from Modrinth, all over HTTPS. Downloads are checked against each source's published checksums. Modpacks can only download from approved hosts, and can't write outside their own folder.

Updates you can trust

Every Embercube update is signed with our Ed25519 key, and the app refuses to install anything that isn't. A tampered download or a fake update server can't slip something in.

More detail on what is stored and which services Embercube talks to is in the privacy notice.

Fair to creators

Mods are made by people. Embercube sends you to them.

Modrinth pays creators from ads on its website and downloads made in its own app. Downloads from third-party launchers don't count toward those payouts, so Embercube doesn't hide the source.

  • Every project shows its creators, license, links and donation pages.
  • "Visit on Modrinth" opens the project in your browser, where your visit counts.
  • Files download straight from Modrinth's CDN, once, so public download stats stay honest.
  • Embercube identifies itself to Modrinth's API and respects its rate limits.
