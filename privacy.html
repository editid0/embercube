Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026.

Embercube is a desktop app that runs entirely on your computer. It has no servers and no analytics, and it collects no telemetry. The developer receives nothing from your use of it.

What is stored, and where

Data Where
Microsoft sign-in tokens (refresh and access tokens) Encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in the secrets folder of the Embercube data folder. The encryption key is kept in your operating system's credential store: macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, or the Secret Service on Linux.
Account list: Minecraft username, profile UUID, skin URL accounts.json in the Embercube data folder.
Settings, instances, mods, game files, logs The Embercube data folder on your computer.

Removing an account in Embercube deletes its tokens from the credential store. Deleting the data folder removes everything else.

Signing in

Microsoft sign-in uses Microsoft's official device-code flow. You enter a short code on microsoft.com, so Embercube never sees your password. The tokens it receives are used only to get a Minecraft session for launching the game, and they are sent only to Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft services.

Where Embercube connects

Service Why
login.microsoftonline.com, user.auth.xboxlive.com, xsts.auth.xboxlive.com, api.minecraftservices.com Signing in and loading your Minecraft profile.
piston-meta.mojang.com, launchermeta.mojang.com, libraries.minecraft.net, resources.download.minecraft.net Game versions, libraries, assets and Java runtimes from Mojang.
api.modrinth.com, cdn.modrinth.com Searching and downloading mods, modpacks, resource packs and shaders.
meta.fabricmc.net, meta.quiltmc.org, maven.fabricmc.net, maven.quiltmc.org, maven.minecraftforge.net, files.minecraftforge.net, maven.neoforged.net Installing mod loaders.
api.adoptium.net, github.com Downloading Java where Mojang doesn't provide a build for your system.

Requests identify the app with a User-Agent of the form EmbercubeMC/embercube/<version>, as Modrinth's API asks. Links you open, such as Modrinth project pages, open in your own browser under that site's policies.
